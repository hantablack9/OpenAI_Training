# OpenAI Cyber Solutions Practitioner

## Course story

This track focuses on qualification and routing. Understand the defensive workflow, its signal source, where work slows, who reviews it, and what proof would show improvement before discussing access or specialized capability.

## In plain English

Do not lead with the most advanced-sounding cyber capability. Lead with the customer’s authorized workflow, the evidence they need, and the safest route that can prove useful progress.

## Interview lens

Keep three decisions separate: context, agentic runtime, and observability. Route to the safest appropriate path, preserve the customer’s system of record, and escalate when authorization, sensitivity, access, or evidence is unclear.

## Product-fact caution

The official models catalog now includes OpenAI Daybreak cyber models, but a catalog entry is not a user or customer entitlement. Treat Trusted Access and related routing language as approval-process guidance; verify eligibility, access, pricing, retention, and deployment terms through the approved OpenAI process. See the [official models catalog](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models).
