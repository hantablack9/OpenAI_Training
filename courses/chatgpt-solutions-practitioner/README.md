# ChatGPT Solutions Practitioner

## Course story

Begin with workflow pain, not the label “chatbot” or “copilot.” Identify the primary user and output, then separate the product surface, required capability, and operating model. ChatGPT may fit collaborative thinking and drafting; delegated multi-step work, software delivery, or an embedded system may point elsewhere.

## In plain English

First understand where the work happens and what the user needs at the end. Only then decide whether ChatGPT, Codex, the API, or a combination is the sensible fit.

## Interview lens

Explain the trigger, pain, affected people, current workaround, business consequence, desired output, governance requirements, and next evidence-building step. Do not jump from interest to a deployment promise.
