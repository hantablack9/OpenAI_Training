# ChatGPT Solutions Practitioner — concise notes

- Start with workflow pain, not product labels such as “chatbot,” “copilot,” or “AI assistant.” Apply the primary-output test: what should the AI return, change, complete, or synthesize?
- Strong ChatGPT-fit patterns include knowledge access, work acceleration, team consistency, and decision support for business users working with approved context and human review.
- Route by three layers: primary user/product surface, required capabilities, and operating model. ChatGPT Chat fits collaborative thinking and creation; ChatGPT Work fits delegated, bounded multi-step knowledge work; Codex fits software engineering; API fits embedded product/system experiences; combined paths fit cross-surface workflows.
- Frame the problem with: trigger, specific pain, affected people, current workaround, business consequence, decision/deliverable at stake, and desired better state.
- Read buyer context: business leaders care about value and impact; operators care about ease and usefulness; IT/security care about governance, access, boundaries, feasibility, and review.
- Before deeper progression, identify stakeholders, approved sources, permissions, review ownership, success signals, and the highest-priority validation gap. Do not route or promise deployment too early.
- A fit-check judgment should state: workflow/output fit, why ChatGPT Chat or Work is the likely first experience, and what validation remains.
