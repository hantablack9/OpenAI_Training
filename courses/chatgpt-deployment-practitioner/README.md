# ChatGPT Deployment Practitioner

## Course story

Deployment readiness means that the customer problem, first cohort, owners, success evidence, workspace/control dependencies, rollout motion, and unresolved risks are concrete enough for a responsible decision. Separate facts, assumptions, and unknowns; then choose a pilot, phased rollout, validation, re-scope, or defer motion.

## In plain English

A good deployment plan tells a team who starts, what they try, how success is measured, who supports it, and what would make them pause or change direction.

## Interview lens

Describe the Deployment Brief: workflow problem, first users, owners, intended ChatGPT experience, success thresholds, scope, risks, observability/review ownership, and immediate next step.
