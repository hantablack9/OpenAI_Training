# OpenAI Technical Practitioner — concise notes

## Program focus

Build technical fluency to route requirements, select the right OpenAI capabilities, shape sound architectures, and account for security, data, governance, and operational constraints from the start.

## Practical framework

1. Start with the customer workflow, users, desired outcome, constraints, and success evidence.
2. Define what is in scope, out of scope, human-owned, and still unknown.
3. Choose the simplest capability and architecture that fits: direct generation, structured output, retrieval grounding, tools/actions, agents, realtime/multimodal, or a justified hybrid.
4. Treat permissions, data handling, source ownership/freshness, evaluation, moderation/guardrails, observability, rollback, support, and escalation as design inputs.
5. Make the recommendation reviewable: rationale, assumptions, risks, open questions, owners, and next validation step.

## One-line takeaway

Technical credibility comes from fitting OpenAI capabilities to a bounded, governed workflow and making the trade-offs and readiness evidence explicit.
