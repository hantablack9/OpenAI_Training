# ChatGPT Deployment Practitioner — concise notes

- Deployment readiness principle: confirm the evidence before committing the motion; separate confirmed facts, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Inspect the sales handoff for the customer problem, priority workflow, first user group, business outcome, success evidence, recommended next step, and ChatGPT experience/control dependencies.
- Write a deployment-grade problem statement: current workflow friction, affected users, operational impact, intended ChatGPT-supported workflow, and business outcome.
- Define observable success evidence across adoption/engagement, user and manager feedback, and workflow value; name thresholds, evidence sources, and owners.
- Confirm executive sponsor, business/workflow owner, workspace admin, governance/data owners, decision moment, approval path, and partner deployment lead.
- Keep the first motion narrow: specify in-scope and out-of-scope users, workflows, data, regions, and parked expansion topics; expose unresolved source-access, policy, review, and identity/access dependencies.
- Choose the motion from evidence: proceed, focused validation, targeted re-scope, or no-go/defer; if proceeding, choose pilot, phased rollout, or broader planning.
- A Deployment Brief should capture problem, first workflow/cohort, owners, intended ChatGPT experience and dependencies, success criteria, scope, assumptions, recommendation, rollout structure, open risks, observability/review ownership, and immediate next step.
- Keep the brief concise and evidence-based; avoid unsupported ROI, feature lists, premature setup details, and promises of automation without human review.
- **Course completion:** From Qualified Opportunity to Deployment Plan completed at 52/52 (100%).
