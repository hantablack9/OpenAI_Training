# Codex Solutions Practitioner — concise notes

- Qualify the workflow behind broad labels such as developer productivity, technical debt, slow testing, or overloaded engineers.
- Strong opportunity signals: recurring software-delivery pain, business impact, credible ownership, readiness, a repeatable workflow, and a concrete task.
- Translate symptoms into a bounded engineering task with accessible context/tools, a target artifact or environment, and clear verification or reviewer criteria.
- Discovery path: identify the outcome under pressure, locate the workflow, test recurrence and impact, identify owner/readiness, then test task viability.
- Capture confirmed evidence, working hypotheses, qualification implications, and evidence gaps.
- Progress when evidence supports deeper discussion; continue discovery when pain is real but task, owner, readiness, or impact is incomplete; pause when urgency/readiness is weak; disqualify unrelated problems or weak software-delivery fit.
- Route detailed deployment, security, governance, and access questions to the appropriate follow-up rather than over-positioning during qualification.
